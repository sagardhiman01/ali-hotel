Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Dargah Sabir Pak?

Ali Hotel is just walking distance (~500 meters) from the holy Dargah Sharif, making it very easy for elders and families to attend all daily prayers and ziyarat.

Is vehicle parking available?

Yes, safe and dedicated vehicle parking is available on-site for both personal cars and group traveler buses/vans.

Is AC and Power Backup available 24/7?

Yes, all 18 rooms are equipped with split air-conditioning supported by uninterrupted generator and inverter backup.

What are the Check-in & Check-out times?

We provide flexible 24-hour check-in and check-out to accommodate pilgrims and travelers arriving at any hour of the day or night.
