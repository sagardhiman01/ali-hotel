Complete Facilities

Amenities Built for Your Comfort

100% Air Conditioned

All 18 guest rooms feature high-efficiency split AC units to ensure comfortable climate control during hot summer months.

24/7 Power Backup

Heavy-duty generator and inverter power backup running continuously so your AC and lights never turn off.

Modern Elevator / Lift

High-speed passenger lift connecting all floors, making room access effortless for elderly pilgrims and families with luggage.

Safe On-Site Parking

Convenient parking space right at the hotel for cars, buses, and traveler vans with 24/7 watch.

High-Speed Free Wi-Fi

Complimentary high-speed internet available throughout all rooms and corridors for your work and family calls.

Geyser Hot & Cold Water

Instant hot water geysers and 24-hour continuous fresh running water in every private attached bathroom.

Daily Sanitized Cleaning

Strict hygiene protocols with fresh clean bedsheets, sanitized blankets, and spotless bathroom maintenance daily.

LED TV & Entertainment

Wall-mounted LED TV in guest rooms with multiple channels for relaxation after visiting Dargah Sharif.

24-Hour Helpdesk & Service

Attentive hotel staff and owners available around the clock to assist you with room service and ziyarat guidelines.
