About Ali Hotel

A trusted haven of peace, family comfort, and authentic hospitality.

Our Legacy

Hospitality Built on Care & Devotion

Ali Hotel is a premier luxury & family stay located on Sohalpur Road near Jhanda Chowk, Piran Kaliyar, Roorkee (Uttarakhand). Managed under the esteemed guidance of Haji Azad Ali, Mohd Shadab, and Haji Abdul Samad, our property is tailored specifically for families, pilgrim groups (Zairin), and leisure travelers visiting the sacred Dargah of Hazrat Alauddin Ali Ahmed Sabir Kalyari (Sabir Pak).

With 18 thoughtfully equipped AC rooms, we offer comfortable lodging with individual 3-bed, 5-bed, and 12-bed configurations to suit every family and group size with top-class cleanliness and safety.

Why Choose Us

Your Peaceful Retreat Near Dargah Sharif

Visiting a holy place calls for a peaceful, worry-free stay. At Ali Hotel, we ensure that every guest experiences true tranquility:

  • 18 Fully Air-Conditioned Rooms with modern bedding and split ACs.
  • 24/7 Power Backup & Hot Geysers in all attached bathrooms.
  • Secure On-Premise Parking for personal cars and traveler vans.
  • Just 500 Meters from Dargah Sharif with easy access to market and transport.
