Welcome to Ali Hotel

Located near VIP Chowk on Sohalpur Road, Piran Kaliyar Shareef (Roorkee), Ali Hotel offers world-class hospitality, tranquil ambiance, and modern air-conditioned accommodations for families, groups, and zairin.
