Welcome to Ali Hotel

Located near Jhanda Chowk on Sohalpur Road, Piran Kaliyar (Roorkee), Ali Hotel offers world-class hospitality, tranquil ambiance, and modern air-conditioned accommodations for families, groups, and zairin.

With 18 fully furnished AC rooms ranging from 3-Bed Deluxe to 12-Bed Grand Family Suites, we ensure complete comfort, round-the-clock hot water, uninterrupted power backup, and dedicated owner assistance.
